Handy to know

  • In the 3D view: drag to orbit, Ctrl + scroll (or pinch) to zoom, click or tap a fixture or its number to select it. "Fullscreen" gives the stage the whole screen.
  • Drag over the fixture tiles to select several at once – hold Shift to add.
  • Double-click or double-tap a cue to play it. Manual values stay on top of cues until you clear them.
  • Drag the faders or use the arrow keys. The Mix fader fades the cue level.

What the real program adds

  • Real fixtures from the GDTF library and your own 3D stages with their models.
  • DMX output over Art-Net, sACN and USB – plus timecode, media, remote control and more panels.
  • The command line with its full command set and the Effect Studio with its script language.
