GDTF Share (online service) and GDTF fixture files

OpenCue does NOT bundle any fixture files from GDTF Share. Its fixture library can download GDTF files from GDTF Share (https://gdtf-share.com) when the user asks for them
(free account required); the user installs them into their own data folder.

* GDTF (General Device Type Format) is an open standard: DIN SPEC 15800:2022 (https://github.com/mvrdevelopment/spec).
* GDTF Share is hosted by the German Entertainment Technology Association (VPLT). Use of the service is subject to its Terms and Conditions:
  https://gdtf-share.com/landing/pages/termsAndConditions.php
* Rights in an uploaded fixture file stay with the uploader / manufacturer (title and intellectual property rights belong to the content owner). The terms allow downloading
  for personal use, do not allow modifying or copying the content beyond what they permit, and require a licence for commercial use of the materials. For that reason OpenCue
  ships no GDTF files of its own selection; fixtures that come with OpenCue are the three built-in generic devices (Generic Dimmer, Generic RGBW LED Par,
  Generic Moving Head Spot 16ch), which are part of OpenCue itself.
* A fixture file you download and a show that contains such a fixture are your own responsibility under the terms of the file's owner and of GDTF Share.
